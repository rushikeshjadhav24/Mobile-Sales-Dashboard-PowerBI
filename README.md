# 📱 Mobile Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The Mobile Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze mobile phone sales performance and provide meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps users understand sales trends, transaction patterns, quantity sold, brand performance, and payment method preferences.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze mobile sales data and create an interactive dashboard that helps businesses:

- Track total sales performance
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare sales across different brands
- Analyze units sold by brand
- Understand customer payment preferences
- Identify high-performing brands
- Monitor overall transaction and quantity metrics

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Total Sale by Month

A monthly trend analysis showing how mobile sales changed throughout the year.

### 2. Total Sale by Brand

A brand-wise comparison of total sales for:

- Apple
- OnePlus
- Vivo
- Samsung
- Xiaomi

### 3. Sum of Units Sold by Brand

Shows the number of mobile units sold by each brand.

### 4. Total Quantity by Brand

A donut chart representing the contribution of each brand to total quantity sold.

### 5. Transaction by Payment Method

Analyzes transactions based on:

- Credit Card
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Cash

### 6. Monthly Filters

Users can interact with the dashboard using month filters from January to December.

## 🔍 Key Insights

From the dashboard:

- Total sales reached approximately **308M**.
- Around **8K units** were sold.
- The dashboard recorded approximately **2K transactions**.
- Apple generated the highest sales among the displayed brands.
- Credit Card was the most frequently used payment method.
- Monthly sales varied throughout the year, with some months showing significantly higher sales than others.
- Brand-wise quantity and sales performance can be compared interactively.
